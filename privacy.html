Privacy Policy for InkPDF

Effective date: August 14, 2026 · Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InkPDF ("we", "us", or "our") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the InkPDF Android application (the "App"). InkPDF is a local document reader and editor for PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files.

1. Documents and Content

The App may access documents in either of these ways:

  • Files you choose: When you open or import a document through the Android file picker, share sheet, or another application.
  • Optional device scan: When you explicitly enable broad file access and start a device scan, the App searches accessible storage for supported PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files so it can show them in the document library.

Document contents, annotations, signatures, form entries, OCR text, and converted files are processed locally on your device. InkPDF does not upload your documents or their contents to an InkPDF server.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Recently opened document references, favorites, sorting preferences, language, and reader settings.
  • Imported files, generated documents, annotations, signatures, OCR results, conversions, and crash-recovery drafts.
  • Local crash logs containing the app version, device model, Android version, thread name, and technical stack trace. These logs remain on your device unless you choose to export and share them.

Some exported or converted documents are saved in shared folders such as Downloads, Documents, or Pictures and remain there until you delete them.

3. Third-Party Services

The App includes or may use the following services:

  • Google Play services and ML Kit: Used for document scanning and on-device text recognition (OCR). Required components or recognition models may be downloaded by Google Play services. Google may process technical service information under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to understand App usage and improve reliability. It may process app interactions, device and operating-system information, app version, approximate region, and identifiers provided by Google services. It does not receive document contents from InkPDF.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Used to receive crash diagnostics such as stack traces, app state, app version, device model, operating-system version, and crash-related identifiers. It does not intentionally receive document contents.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Used to retrieve non-document App configuration. It is never used to upload document contents.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are governed by Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google Privacy Policy. The current version does not display advertising, does not use AdMob, and does not enable paid subscriptions. If advertising, document cloud services, or subscriptions are enabled in a future version, this policy and the relevant in-app disclosures will be updated before activation.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To find and display supported documents when you request a scan.
  • To open, render, edit, annotate, sign, organize, convert, and export documents.
  • To provide OCR, document scanning, recovery, recent-file, and favorite features.
  • To diagnose crashes from logs stored locally on your device.
  • To measure App usage, diagnose remotely reported crashes, improve reliability, and retrieve non-document configuration through Firebase.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or document contents. Documents leave the App only when you choose an Android action such as Share, Open with, Export, or Save to a location managed by another application. Google and Firebase may receive the technical, analytics, crash, and service data described above according to their privacy terms, but InkPDF does not intentionally send document contents to Firebase.

6. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • All files access / storage access: Optional access used to locate and manage supported documents across shared storage. You may decline it and continue opening individual files through the Android file picker.
  • Internet: Used to open the official website and privacy policy, download Google service components when required, and retrieve optional remote configuration.

7. Retention and Deletion

Recent-file records and app settings remain until you clear them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. App-private imports, drafts, recent-file references, favorites, bookmarks, signatures, and local crash logs are excluded from InkPDF's Android backup rules. Limited non-sensitive interface preferences may be restored through Android backup or device transfer. Files exported to shared storage must be deleted by you through InkPDF or a file manager. Firebase data retention is governed by Google's applicable settings and policies. You can revoke storage access at any time in Android Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

  • Open individual files without enabling broad storage access.
  • Revoke storage access in Android Settings.
  • Clear recent records and local app data.
  • Delete exported files from shared storage.
  • Contact us with privacy questions or requests.

10. Security

Document processing is designed to occur locally. We use Android storage permissions, URI grants, and app-private storage to limit access where practical. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your device, exported files, and document passwords.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App's update release notes.
